Decision rules

Keep: CAA when it creates a 4th–5th-grade opportunity IYAC cannot provide.
Review manually: every 6th-grade CAA placement that overlaps an IYAC opportunity. Sixth grade is the bridge, not an automatic CAA assignment.
5th grade: technically IYAC eligible in many member programs, but Explore should keep fifth graders on elementary teams whenever feasible.
Participation-dependent: do not pay a CAA team fee until enrollment clears a roster viability threshold you are comfortable coaching.
