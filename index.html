Placement Rules

Roster logic

Eligibility is not the same thing as best placement. The portal should help make developmental decisions, not blindly sort children by the broadest league rule.

4th

CAA elementary by default. IYAC is not the primary pathway.

5th

Keep with elementary teams whenever possible. Use IYAC only when appropriate or necessary.

6th

True bridge grade. Place based on enrollment, competitive balance, safety, and team viability.

7–8th

IYAC first whenever IYAC offers the sport. Avoid unnecessary CAA team fees.
